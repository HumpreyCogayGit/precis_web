Turn 2 — Filters that don't grow

Stop rendering the taxonomy in the header

A chip row is a control whose size is your database. It works to about eight items and breaks after that — which is what you're hitting. The fix in all three options is the same move: the header holds a constant-width control, and the taxonomy moves into a surface that can scroll. Active filters then appear as chips, but as output, not as the control.

On your filter bug
The distortion is almost certainly filter chips wrapping and stretching to fill the row. Fix: one row, overflow-x:auto, white-space:nowrap, flex:none on every chip, and 16–18px horizontal page padding that the scroll area bleeds past. Chips keep their intrinsic width and the row scrolls instead of reflowing.
